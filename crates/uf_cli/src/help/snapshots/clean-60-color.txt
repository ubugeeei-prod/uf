\e[1;96muf clean\e[0m
\e[90m────────\e[0m
Remove what a rebuild would write again.

The build's output, uf's own per-project state under \e[96m.uf/\e[0m,
and the caches. Not \e[96mnode_modules\e[0m unless asked, and never a
lockfile: the line is the network, and a lockfile is an
input.

\e[1mUsage\e[0m
  \e[96muf\e[0m \e[96mclean\e[0m \e[90m[OPTIONS]\e[0m

\e[1mOptions\e[0m

             round trip

  \e[96m--dry-run\e[0m  Print what would be removed, and remove nothing

\e[1mGlobal options\e[0m
      \e[96m--cwd\e[0m \e[90m<DIR>\e[0m     Run as if uf had been started in DIR
